# Atelier Demo Video Script (60–90 Seconds)

**Target Runtime:** 75 seconds  
**Tone:** Confident, deliberate, design-engineered. Calm ambient studio soundtrack.

---

### [00:00 - 00:15] Hook: Persona, Storefront & Design Craft
- **Visual:** Smooth scroll down the Atelier home page at `1280px`. Camera pauses on the warm linen textures, Playfair Display typography, and WCAG AA contrast tokens.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"This is Atelier—an artisanal ecommerce store for considered home and outdoor goods. I built this to demonstrate how full-stack Next.js 15, PostgreSQL with pgvector, and grounded artificial intelligence come together with high design craft."*

---

### [00:15 - 00:35] Act I: Grounded AI Personal Curator
- **Visual:** Click the floating "Ask Assistant" pill. The slide-out curator opens. Click prompt: *"Recommend a pour-over dripper for morning coffee."* Assistant streams a grounded reply, displaying an interactive product card for the *Hand-Thrown Stoneware Dripper* ($44.00) with real stock badges. Click *"Add to Cart"*. Cart badge increments optimistically.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Rather than a hallucinating gimmick, Atelier's AI assistant is strictly grounded in database inventory. It cross-checks live stock, refuses prompt injection attempts, and returns actual interactive UI cards that link directly into the transaction pipeline."*

---

### [00:35 - 00:50] Act II: Hybrid Semantic Search with Reciprocal Rank Fusion
- **Visual:** Type in search bar: *"something warm for hiking in the rain"*. Press Enter. Search results populate instantly (under 60ms latency). Point out the active filter chips and the *"Why this matched"* card note explaining the Martexin waxed canvas weatherproofing.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Our search engine combines 768-dimensional vector cosine distance with PostgreSQL full-text rank using Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Natural language queries like 'something warm for hiking in the rain' surface exact in-stock matches with transparent reasoning."*

---

### [00:50 - 01:05] Act III: Admin Studio & AI Artisan Copywriter
- **Visual:** Switch into `/admin`. Show the real-time revenue and low-stock dashboard. Click *"Draft New Product with AI"*. Type a short artisan note: *"Hand-pinched coarse volcanic clay bowl"*. Click *"Draft with AI"*. Form fills instantly with editable title, narrative copy, tags, and SEO alt text.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"For shop managers, the admin suite turns raw artisan notes into editable, production-ready product copy and automatically indexes vector embeddings upon publication."*

---

### [01:05 - 01:15] Close: Automated Evals & Architecture
- **Visual:** Cut to terminal showing `npm run eval` passing with a 90% Hit@5 rate and 100% injection defense. End on the clean repository README and live demo link.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every AI feature is backed by automated eval suites, strict TypeScript, and end-to-end Stripe checkout. Atelier is fully open-source and live. Thank you."*
