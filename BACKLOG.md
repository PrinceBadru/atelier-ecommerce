# Feature Backlog & Future Improvements

This backlog captures features and ideas proposed outside the core requirements of `PLAN.md`.

- [ ] **Multi-currency display:** Automatic localization of currencies with real-time FX rate conversion.
- [ ] **Multi-vendor marketplace:** Vendor portal for third-party artisans to onboard products.
- [ ] **Augmented Reality (WebXR) preview:** 3D model visualization for home goods and furniture in mobile AR.
- [ ] **Voice-enabled assistant:** Real-time WebRTC audio conversation with Gemini Live for hands-free shopping.
- [ ] **Automated inventory restock prediction:** AI time-series forecasting based on browse events and order velocity.
